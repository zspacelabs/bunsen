Every recognizer here starts with the same few steps: short overlapping windows of the waveform, a power spectrum of each, a bank of triangular filters spaced on the mel scale, and a logarithm. The kits differ in the log's base and in the last step, the level each frame is measured against, and that last step decides whether a front end can stream exactly.

The log-mel spectrogram

  • Windows. 25 ms windows (400 samples at 16 kHz) every 10 ms: 100 frames a second, in every kit.
  • Power spectrum. A Hann-windowed FFT, squared magnitude. Whisper's window is periodic and its padding a reflection; NeMo's (Parakeet) is symmetric, zero-padded to 512, after a 0.97 preemphasis filter that tilts the spectrum toward high frequencies.
  • Mel bands. 80 or 128 triangular filters, spaced evenly in mels: finely at low frequencies, where pitch and formants live, coarsely above. Whisper large-v3 moved from 80 to 128 bands, and the later kits all use 128.
  • Log. Loudness is perceived roughly logarithmically, and a log turns a gain into an additive offset. Whisper and its descendants use log₁₀ over a 10⁻¹⁰ floor; NeMo uses the natural log over 2⁻²⁴.

Levels

The last step decides what "loud" means:

  • Whisper floors every value at 8 below the clip's maximum (an 80 dB range in log₁₀ units of power), then maps x → (x + 4)/4{{cite:radford2022}}. The maximum is over the whole clip, which a stream doesn't have yet; bunsen's driver uses the loudest frame heard so far, or each window's own.
  • Qwen3-ASR uses Whisper's rule over each 30 s window.
  • Voxtral Realtime uses Whisper's large-v3 front end but against a fixed level (1.5). No statistic of the audio enters, so a stream's features are the same however it is chunked, which a causal model needs.
  • Parakeet standardizes each mel channel to zero mean and unit variance over the item. That is the most robust to recording conditions, and the least streamable: a stream normalizes each window by its own moments.

ZONOS3's speaker encoder uses a third family's front end, Kaldi's 80-band filterbank with its mean subtracted, since it was trained in a speaker-recognition toolkit.
