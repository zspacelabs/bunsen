A unit kind is the shape of the residual path F: how many convs it has, at what widths, and which of them is the spatial mixer. All the kinds lower to the same generic unit (a chain of convs with one marked as the mixer), so this chapter is about widths and orderings. What the mixer itself computes is covered by the conv and mixer chapters.

Basic

Two 3×3 convs at the stream width. The first one strides and is the mixer{{cite:he2016}}. It is cheap at small widths and expensive at large ones, since every conv is 9C². ResNet-18/34, ResNetV2-18/34 and SKNet-18/34 use it.

Bottleneck

A 1×1 reduce, a 3×3 mixer at a quarter of the width, and a 1×1 expand back (expansion 4){{cite:he2016}}. The spatial mixing, which is the expensive part, happens in a narrow space, and the residual stream can be wide. A ResNet-50 stage carries 256 to 2048 channels but mixes in 64 to 512.

Two refinements come with the lineage. Stride on the 3×3 (torchvision's default, "ResNet-B"): striding the first 1×1 would discard ¾ of the input (see shortcuts), so the stride moves to the mixer{{cite:he2019tricks}}. Grouping the mixer (ResNeXt): the mixer width is ⌊planes · base_width / 64⌋ · groups. A ResNet-50 is 1×64, a ResNeXt-50 32×4d is 32 groups of 4, and a Wide ResNet-50-2 is 1×128.

Grouped bottleneck as RegNet states it

RegNet sets the expansion with a bottleneck ratio b (mixer width = unit width × b) and states groups by their size rather than their count{{cite:radosavovic2020}}. RegNetX/Y use b = 1: no narrowing at all, so all of the saving comes from the grouped mixer.

Inverted

With b > 1 the path widens instead of narrowing: expand → mix → project, as in MobileNetV2 {{cite:sandler2018}}. RegNetZ uses b = 4 with a narrow residual stream. A narrow stream carries little information, so rectifying it would destroy some of it, which is why inverted units are paired with linear outputs{{cite:dollar2021}}.

Pre-activated

The basic and bottleneck paths again, with each conv's norm and activation moved before it and the unit's output activation removed (pre- versus post-activation). The path's convs are unchanged. Only the boundary between units moves{{cite:he2016id}}.
