DeiT{{cite:touvron2021deit}} answered ViT's data problem with training rather than architecture. A ViT-B trained on ImageNet-1k alone, with strong augmentation, regularization and a long schedule, matched the convnets of its day. The plain DeiTs are ViTs, slot for slot. The architectural contribution is the distillation token. DeiT III{{cite:touvron2022deit3}} later improved the recipe and added layer scale.

DeiT, and distillation through attention

Design change. A second prefix token, the distillation token, follows the class token. A second classifier reads it and is trained against a teacher's predictions, while the class token's classifier is trained against the true label. At inference the two classifiers' logits are averaged.

Why. Knowledge distillation{{cite:hinton2015}} usually adds a loss on the student's single output. Giving the teacher its own token lets the network keep two related but different summaries. In the paper, the class and distillation tokens start out nearly orthogonal and become similar but not identical by the last layer. A conv teacher worked better than a transformer teacher, which suggests the student inherits some of the conv's inductive bias through the labels. Hard-label distillation beat soft.

Slot fill. tokens = class + distillation + positions over both · pool = distilled · head = two classifiers, averaged. The non-distilled DeiTs need no circuit of their own: they are the ViT circuit.

DeiT III

Design change. Layer scale on every residual{{cite:touvron2021cait}}, and a position embedding over the patches alone. No distillation.

Why. DeiT III's subject is again training: a simpler three-augmentation recipe, a binary cross-entropy loss, simple random crops, and a fresh look at ImageNet-21k pretraining. With it a plain supervised ViT-H trains well and matches self-supervised pretraining{{cite:touvron2022deit3}}. Layer scale, from the same group's CaiT, is what keeps the larger and deeper models stable. A medium shape (width 512) fills the gap between S and B.
