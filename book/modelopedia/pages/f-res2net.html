Res2Net{{cite:gao2019res2}} is a ResNet whose bottleneck mixer is a scale split: the middle channels are cut into s chunks and convolved in a hierarchy, so one unit mixes at receptive fields 1, 3, 5, … 2s−1. Everything outside the mixer (stem, depths, shortcuts, head) is a ResNet's.

Design

Change. Mixer = Res2 scale split of s chunks, each w wide, where w is the ResNet mixer width per 64 planes. The 1×1 reduce widens to s·w channels per 64 planes (104 for 26w_4s, against a ResNet's 64).

Why. CNNs had gained multi-scale representations only between layers (the stage pyramid, FPN). Res2Net adds them inside a layer at almost no cost. Scale s becomes a new dimension next to depth, width and cardinality, and the paper reports accuracy gains from raising it at a matched budget.

Slot fill. unit = bottleneck (only) · mixer = Res2(s, w). The D variants (res2net50d) add the ResNet-D deep stem and avg-down shortcut.

The design space is the pair (w, s), spelled 26w_4s, 14w_8s, 48w_2s: a trade between chunk width and hierarchy length at a roughly constant s·w. These are scale settings of one design, not separate families.

Res2NeXt

The scale split's branch convs grouped by a cardinality. This crosses Res2 with ResNeXt: the two axes compose because they act on different structure (chunks across the hierarchy, groups within a chunk).
