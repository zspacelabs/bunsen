Parakeet TDT is NVIDIA's transducer recognizer{{cite:parakeet2025}}: a FastConformer encoder read by a token-and-duration transducer. It has no attention between audio and text, and no large language model; its decoder is a two-layer LSTM, the smallest and simplest decoder among bunsen's recognizers.

Parakeet TDT

Design. 128 natural-log mel bands after preemphasis, standardized per band. An 8× dw-striding stem to 80 ms frames. 24 FastConformer blocks of width 1024 (8 heads, kernel-9 convolutions, no biases) with relative positions. A projection to 640, a prediction network of two 640-wide LSTMs over an 8192-piece SentencePiece vocabulary plus blank, and a joint that scores tokens and durations of 0 to 4 frames.

Why. The Conformer's convolution gives the encoder the local acoustic modelling attention lacks, and FastConformer's 8× subsampling cuts attention's cost by 4× against the original{{cite:rekesh2023}}. A transducer decodes in step with the audio, so it is far less prone than an attention decoder to writing text the audio doesn't contain, and TDT's durations let it skip the frames where nothing new is said{{cite:xu2023tdt}}. Every token comes out with its frame, so word timestamps cost nothing. v3 covers 25 European languages.
