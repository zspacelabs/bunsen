Qwen3-TTS{{cite:qwen2025tts}} turns the Qwen3 decoder into a speech synthesizer. A talker, a Qwen3 stack, writes one codec frame per step, but only its first codebook. A five-layer depth decoder fills in the other fifteen, and a 12.5 Hz codec decodes the codes to 24 kHz audio.

Qwen3-TTS

Design.

  • Rows. The prompt is chat-style text (an optional instruction, the assistant role) followed by codec control rows: a thinking tag, a language, a speaker. Each prompt row is a text embedding plus a codec embedding; the text table (Qwen3's) reaches the talker's width through a small MLP. Each generated frame's row is the sum of all sixteen codes' embeddings and the next text embedding.
  • Talker. A Qwen3 stack (28 × 1024 at 0.6B) with its own head over codebook 0 and the control rows. Its release config asks for multimodal RoPE, but with text-only positions every section rotates alike, so bunsen applies an ordinary 1-d RoPE.
  • Depth decoder. A 5-layer Qwen3 stack that, per frame, reads the talker's hidden state and codebook 0's embedding and writes codebooks 1 to 15, each with its own table and head.
  • Codec. A split RVQ (one semantic codebook, fifteen acoustic, 2048 codes each), a causal conv, an 8-layer windowed transformer with layer scale, two ×2 upsamplers, and a DAC vocoder with Snake activations: 1920 samples per frame.

Why. Predicting codebook 0 with the big model and the rest with a small one puts the capacity where the content is: the semantic codebook carries what is said and how, and the acoustic ones are largely determined by it. The talker runs at 12.5 steps a second, the depth decoder's cost is fixed per frame, and the codec is causal, so synthesis can stream.

Variants

The three releases share their codec and sampling settings. The 1.7B talker is 2048 wide, so its depth decoder gains an input projection to its own 1024. CustomVoice models speak in nine stock voices, each a speaker row; the 1.7B also takes an instruction about style. VoiceDesign has no speaker rows: the voice is described in words, as the instruction. Neither is architecture beyond the prompt.
