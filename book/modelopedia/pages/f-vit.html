The Vision Transformer{{cite:dosovitskiy2020}} took the text Transformer's encoder{{cite:vaswani2017}}, in its pre-norm form, almost unchanged and fed it image patches as words. Its result was about scale, not architecture. On ImageNet alone a ViT trails a ResNet of similar size, because it lacks the conv's locality bias. Pretrained on hundreds of millions of images it overtakes the ResNet, because data can teach what the conv's structure had built in. Every family in this half descends from it.

ViT

Design. 16×16 (or 32, 14, 8) patches become width-D tokens. A class token goes in front, and a learnt position embedding covers all of them. Then come L identical pre-norm blocks of global multi-head attention and a GELU MLP (×4), a final LayerNorm, and the class token read by a linear classifier.

Why. The point was to use as little vision-specific structure as possible. Apart from cutting the image into patches (and resizing the position embedding to fine-tune at a higher resolution), the model knows nothing about 2-d images, and the paper shows that this is enough at scale. The shapes come from BERT: Base is 12 layers of 768 with 12 heads, Large 24 of 1024 with 16, Huge 32 of 1280 with 16. timm adds Tiny and Small, and later towers add their own (so400m, giant). Those are scale, not design.

Slot fill. patch = p×p conv with bias · tokens = class + learnt positions over all · blocks = LN → MHSA, LN → MLP · final = LN · pool = token.

Hybrid ViT: a ResNetV2 trunk

Design change. The patch embedding cuts a conv net's feature map instead of the image. In R50+ViT-B/16 the trunk is ResNet-50's stem and first three stages, the third lengthened from 6 units to 9. Its stride-16 map is cut into 1×1 patches, so the token grid is the same as a plain ViT-B/16's{{cite:dosovitskiy2020}}. The trunk is the ResNetV2 kit used without pre-activation: BiT's group norm and weight-standardized convs with SAME padding, and no head. The smaller hybrids from How to train your ViT cut other maps: R26+ViT-S/32 has four stages of two units, and the Ti hybrid keeps the stem alone and cuts its map in 8×8 patches{{cite:steiner2021}}.

ResPost ViT

A timm experiment{{cite:timm}}: ViT blocks in Swin V2's res-post-norm placement, with no class token, an average pool normalized after pooling, and no qkv bias. It tests on a plain ViT the stability fix that Swin V2 introduced for windows.

GAP ViTs

timm's "searching for better baselines" ViTs{{cite:timm}} (medium, betwixt, mediumd and others) drop the class token and average the patch tokens. They add layer scale and remove the qkv bias. The plain _gap_ nets keep the final norm before the pool. Their register siblings are covered in DINOv2 & Registers.

FlexiViT

FlexiViT{{cite:beyer2023flexivit}} trains one ViT over randomly varying patch sizes, resizing the patch-embedding weights and position embedding to match, so a single model serves every compute budget. What it leaves in the weights' structure is a position embedding over the patches alone (no_embed_class). The kit builds each FlexiViT at its trained patch size, 16.
