DINOv2 is the reference self-supervised ViT: a general-purpose feature extractor trained without labels. Its follow-up found a defect in large ViTs' feature maps and fixed it with register tokens. Several later towers (C-RADIO, timm's register baselines) inherit registers and layer scale. In the algebra, this lineage changes three sockets: TOKENS, LAYER SCALE and, at giant size, the MLP.

DINOv2

Design change. Layer scale on every residual, 14×14 patches at 518 pixels (a 37×37 grid), and at giant size a packed SwiGLU MLP.

Why. DINOv2 trains by self-distillation: a student matches an exponential-moving-average teacher across augmented views, at both the image and the patch level, on a curated dataset of 142M images{{cite:oquab2023dinov2}}. Its architecture choices are the ones that keep that recipe stable at 1B parameters: layer scale from CaiT, and SwiGLU for the giant model.

Registers

Design change. k extra learnt prefix tokens after the class token, read by nothing. Positions cover the patches alone.

Why. Darcet et al. traced spikes in DINOv2's (and CLIP's, and DeiT III's) attention maps to a few background patch tokens that had stopped describing their patch and instead held global information{{cite:darcet2024registers}}. Adding even a handful of register tokens moves that role off the grid. The feature and attention maps become clean, and dense downstream tasks improve. The reg4 in a name is the count.

C-RADIO

Design change. 7 or 9 registers, positions over the patches, and no final norm. The base and large towers keep layer scale; the huge and giant ones drop it.

Why. AM-RADIO distills several foundation models at once (CLIP-style, DINOv2 and SAM teachers) into one student. The student matches each teacher through its own small adaptor head{{cite:ranzinger2024radio}}. C-RADIO (v3, v4) is NVIDIA's continuation. Its towers end at the last block, with no norm, and in the kit's classifiers the pool reads the class token as the last block leaves it.

Register baselines

timm's reg1 and reg4 GAP ViTs{{cite:timm}} combine the GAP baselines with registers: no class token, one or four registers, layer scale and a mean pool, mostly with the norm after the pool. The TIPSv2 towers in the kit are another register geometry: a class token, one register and layer scale. Neither adds a socket beyond those above.
