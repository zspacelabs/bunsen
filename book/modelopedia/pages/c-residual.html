The residual unit is the combinator that gives the lineage its name. Instead of learning a map H(x) directly, a unit learns the residual F(x) = H(x) − x and adds its input back{{cite:he2016}}:

y = act( shortcut(x) + F(x) )

The full slot structure (pre-activation, attention, regularizers) is described in the shared algebra. This chapter covers why the form works and what it computes. The shapes of F are unit kinds; the shapes of the shortcut are shortcuts.

Before residuals

VGG showed that depth built from 3×3 convs pays off up to about 19 layers{{cite:simonyan2014}}. Past that, plain nets got worse on the training set. That is an optimization failure, not overfitting. Highway networks had already added gated skip connections, y = T(x)·H(x) + (1−T(x))·x{{cite:srivastava2015}}. ResNet's step was to remove the gate: the skip is always fully open.

Geometry: perturbations of the identity

This picture explains the optimization benefit. At initialization (and exactly so with the last norm's γ = 0{{cite:goyal2017}}) a deep residual net is close to the identity, which is a good starting point. The Jacobian of a unit is I + ∂F/∂x, so the gradient through a stack of units always contains a direct term that does not shrink with depth.

Post- versus pre-activation

In the generic unit, pre-activation fills the pre slot (one norm and activation over the unit's input, read by both the path and a projection shortcut), turns every later conv into a "norm → act → conv" form, and empties the output-activation slot. A pre-activated net has a bare stem conv and needs a final norm and activation before the pool, because nothing else normalizes the residual stream.

One choice remains: does an identity shortcut read the raw x or the pre-activated one? ResNetV2 and BiT read the raw stream, which is what keeps the identity clean. timm's RegNetV reads the pre-activated input.

Linear outputs and no residual

Two more degenerate cases complete the algebra. A linear-output unit has no activation after the add (RegNetZ, following MobileNetV2's linear bottlenecks{{cite:sandler2018}}), so the residual stream is never rectified, as in pre-activation, but with the norms still after the convs. A unit with no residual is its path alone. RegNetZ uses this where a unit changes shape, instead of spending a projection on a shortcut{{cite:dollar2021}}.
