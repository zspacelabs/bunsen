The Res2 scale split replaces a bottleneck's single 3×3 mixer with a small hierarchy of 3×3s over chunks of the channels{{cite:gao2019res2}}. Each branch sees the previous branch's output, so receptive fields grow inside one unit and a single block holds features at several scales.

The recurrence

y1 = x1,  y2 = K2(x2),  yi = Ki(xi + yi−1)  (2 < i ≤ s)

Inside the mixer this is a residual-style chain along the channel axis. Branch i is a composition of i−1 3×3 convs, so its reach is 2i−1. The output concatenates the identity (reach 1) and every reach up to 2s−1.

Striding units

In a unit that strides or changes width the hierarchy is cut: every convolved chunk is processed separately at the stride, and the pass-through chunk goes through a 3×3 average pool of the same stride so that the shapes agree. The hierarchy exists only in units that keep their shape.

In the algebra

Res2 is a mixer kind for bottleneck units. Its knobs are the scale s and the chunk width w, which is the ResNet bottleneck's ⌊planes · base_width/64⌋ · groups, so 26w_4s means 4 chunks of 26 per 64 planes. Its branches are ordinary conv blocks, grouped in Res2NeXt. DropBlock, if enabled, acts on the concatenated output.
