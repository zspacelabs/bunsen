Two regularizers are built into the conv net's structure rather than added on top: stochastic depth, which drops whole residual paths, and DropBlock, which drops contiguous regions of the mixer's output. Neither has parameters, and both disappear at inference. They are rewrites of the algebra that apply only during training. Plain dropout {{cite:srivastava2014}} only appears before the classifier (head).

Stochastic depth (drop path)

In the ensemble view, drop path trains a random sub-ensemble of short paths on every step and tests on the full product. It needs the residual. A unit with no residual (RegNetZ's reshaping units) cannot be dropped and is skipped.

DropBlock

In the generic unit DropBlock acts after the mixer's norm, before its activation: the hook in the conv block. Each exotic mixer defines where that hook lands. For Res2 it is the concatenated output, for split attention the grouped conv's norm, and for SK the mixer's output.
