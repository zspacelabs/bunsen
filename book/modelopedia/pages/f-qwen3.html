Qwen3 is the plainest modern decoder in the kits: the Llama template, with one socket filled. It is also the most reused: it is the text model of Qwen3-ASR and the talker stack of Qwen3-TTS, and the kit covers every dense Qwen3 checkpoint up to 8B{{cite:yang2025qwen3}}.

Qwen3

Design change. QK-norm: a learned RMS norm on each query and key head, applied before RoPE. Qwen2's biases on the query, key and value projections are gone{{cite:yang2024qwen2}}. Heads are 128 wide at every size, independent of the width (16 × 128 over a 1024-wide model at 0.6B), with GQA from 2:1 to 4:1. RoPE θ = 10⁶. The head is tied to the embedding up to 4B, and the 8B has its own.

Why. The Qwen3 report says the QK-norm replaced the QKV biases to make training stable{{cite:yang2025qwen3}}: it bounds the attention logits (logit control), where the biases had only shifted them. Fixed-width heads keep each head's rank and rotary spectrum the same across sizes. θ = 10⁶ gives the 32K native context room to spare.

Slot fill. embed = plain · norms = RMS ε 10⁻⁶, pre-norm · mixer = GQA, head 128, QK-norm before RoPE · FFN = SwiGLU · head = tied (≤ 4B) or own.

Variants that are not architecture

The kit's checkpoints differ in training, not in circuit. None of them needs a circuit of its own:

  • -Base checkpoints are the pretrained models; the unsuffixed ones are tuned to chat with a thinking mode that the chat template switches on and off.
  • 2507 (Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507, -Thinking-2507) split the two modes into two models and extended the context to 256K. The only structural change is the rotary base, raised to 5·10⁶.
  • Qwen3Guard-Gen (0.6B, 4B, 8B) is Qwen3 tuned to classify a prompt or a reply as Safe, Unsafe or Controversial, with categories, by generating the verdict as text{{cite:qwen2025guard}}. Qwen3-4B-SafeRL is Qwen3-4B tuned for safety by reinforcement learning.
