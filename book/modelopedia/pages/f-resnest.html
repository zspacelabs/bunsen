ResNeSt{{cite:zhang2020resnest}} is a ResNet-D whose bottleneck mixer is split attention: inside each cardinal group, r radix splits are weighed per channel by a softmax attention. Every striding unit downsamples with an average pool (avd) instead of a strided conv.

Design

Change. Mixer = split attention (radix r, cardinality g) · anti-alias = avd 3×3 average pool, after the mixer or before it · stem = deep · shortcut = avg-down.

Why. ResNeSt unifies the attention ideas before it into one operator. SE's channel gating is radix 1. SK's branch selection becomes radix > 1 with same-shaped branches. ResNeXt's cardinality is the outer grouping. The paper's argument is practical: a single drop-in mixer, combined with the D tweaks and a modern training recipe, gives a backbone that transfers well to detection and segmentation.

Slot fill. A ResNet-D (deep stem of 32 or 64, avg-down shortcuts) with mixer = SplitAttn and aa = avd. It cannot also fill the separate attention or anti-aliasing slots: its mixer already plays both roles, and timm's block refuses the combination.

Design variants

The radix·s·cardinality·x·width·d names (1s4x24d, 4s2x40d) trade radix against cardinality. In the radix-1 and radix-4 geometries the avd pool moves before the mixer (avd_first), so the expensive attended conv runs at the reduced resolution. The d and e suffixes mark deep stems of 32 and 64. Depth is scale.
