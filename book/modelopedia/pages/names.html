The lineage's model names (timm's conventions{{cite:timm}}, which bunsen's prefabs keep) are compressed slot assignments. Once the grammar is known, a name reads as a list of slot fills on top of a base. Only the parts that change design are listed below. Depth digits and size labels are scale.

[attention] base [anti-alias] depth [stem/shortcut] [_cardinality x width d]

The transformers' names follow a second grammar:

base _size _patchp [_tower/recipe] [_regk] [_gap] _image

Image models: worked examples

  • resnet50d: ResNet bottlenecks [3, 4, 6, 3] · deep stem 32 · avg-down shortcuts.
  • seresnextaa101d_32x8d: ResNeXt (32 groups of 8) · SE at the output · avg-pool anti-aliasing · deep stem + avg-down · 101 layers. Five slots changed from ResNet v1, each by one token.
  • ecaresnext26t_32x4d: ResNeXt 32×4 · ECA · tiered stem + avg-down · 26 layers of bottlenecks.
  • resnetv2_50x3_bit: pre-activation bottlenecks · GN + WS · widths ×3.
  • resnest50d_4s2x40d: split attention with radix 4 over 2 cardinal groups of 40 · avd before the mixer · deep stem + avg-down.
  • regnety_032: RegNetY (SE at the mixer) at the 3.2 GF point of its width rule.
  • vit_base_r50_s16_384: a ViT-B whose tokens are 1×1 patches of a ResNetV2-50 trunk's stride-16 map, at 384 pixels.
  • vit_so400m_patch14_siglip_gap_384: SigLIP's shape-optimized tower in 14-pixel patches, its attention pool replaced by a mean, at 384 pixels (the 6 pixels past the last whole patch dropped).
  • vit_large_patch14_reg4_dinov2: DINOv2's ViT-L with four registers.
  • swinv2_large_window12to24_192to384: Swin V2-L, pretrained at window 12 and 192 pixels, run at window 24 and 384.

Language and speech checkpoints are named differently: by family, generation, size and tuning, and only some of those words are architecture. Speech checkpoints follow the same grammar as their family (Qwen3-ASR-0.6B, Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-CustomVoice, parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3, whisper large-v3-turbo), with the codec's frame rate or the decoder type as extra words.

Size words sometimes carry a real structural choice, because families vary their structure with size. Gemma 1's 2b uses multi-query attention and 7b full multi-head. Qwen3 ties its head to the embedding up to 4B and gives 8B its own. Gemma 3's 4b doubles the window and scales its global rotary table. The book treats these as parameters of one circuit, and notes them in the family chapters.
