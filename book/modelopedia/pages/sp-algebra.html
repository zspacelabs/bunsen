bunsen's speech kits are language models with a signal path attached at one end. A recognizer (Whisper, Qwen3-ASR, Voxtral Realtime, Parakeet) puts an audio encoder in front of a decoder; a synthesizer (Qwen3-TTS, ZONOS3) puts a codec after one. Between the signal and the decoder sits a bridge, and that is where the recognizers differ most. Inside, the decoders are the ones Part III describes: Qwen3-ASR's text model and Qwen3-TTS's talker are the Qwen3 kit, Voxtral's is a Mistral decoder built from the same block, and ZONOS3's is a gated-DeltaNet hybrid like Qwen3.5's.

Two pipelines, one middle

Both directions need audio turned into a short sequence of vectors: a transformer cannot attend over 16 000 samples a second. Recognizers compute a log-mel spectrogram and subsample it with strided convolutions. Synthesizers use a neural codec, trained separately, whose quantized latents are the tokens the decoder writes. Either way the decoder runs at a frame rate between 12.5 and 50 Hz.

The speech board

The speech board has four rows. AUDIO IN is the front end and audio encoder. BRIDGE is where frames meet tokens: a projector, the bridge itself, and any conditioning. TOKENS is the language model with its input rows, a second decoder if it has one, its heads and its search. AUDIO OUT is the codec decoder. Recognizers fill the top three rows and synthesizers the bottom two, so the two halves of the board read as mirror images. ZONOS3, which can clone a voice from a reference clip, also uses AUDIO IN for its speaker encoder. The LANGUAGE MODEL socket stands for a whole decoder stack, which links to its own circuit on the language-model board where there is one.

What carries over, and what is new

  • The residual algebra carries over. Every audio encoder, codec trunk and speech decoder here is built from the same CausalDecoderBlock and DecoderStack as the language kits, with a mask and a norm placement chosen by config. The Conformer adds a third sublayer kind, a convolution, and ZONOS3 replaces the residual stream itself with routes.
  • Positions come in four new forms. A learned table (Whisper), sinusoids restarted per chunk (Qwen3-ASR), relative positions with Transformer-XL biases (Parakeet), and none at all where convolutions carry order (the codec stems). Rotary positions remain the default in the decoders.
  • Time is a constraint. A stream can't wait for audio it hasn't heard. That decides the front end's level rule, the encoder's mask, the bridge, and the codec's causality, and it is why the kits differ most in exactly those sockets (streams).
  • A frame can be several tokens. A codec emits 10 or 16 codes per frame, so a synthesizer's language model must predict a small structured object at every step (language models over codes).
  • State is injected, here too. Every encoder cache, decoder cache, prediction-network state and codec stream state is made by the caller and passed in, so one loaded model serves many streams.
