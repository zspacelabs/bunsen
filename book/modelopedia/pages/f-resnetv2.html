ResNetV2 is ResNet with every norm and activation moved to the front of its conv{{cite:he2016id}}. BiT keeps that layout and makes it batch-independent with group norm and weight standardization, then scales it for transfer learning{{cite:kolesnikov2020}}. One bunsen kit covers both, plus timm's later pre-activation nets.

Pre-activation ResNet

Design change. Each unit normalizes and activates its input once, and both the path and a projection shortcut read the result. Every later conv in the path is preceded by its own norm and activation. Nothing follows the add, and an identity shortcut carries the raw stream. The stem's last conv is bare, and a final norm and activation runs before the pool.

Why. With a clean identity, xL = xl + ΣFi holds exactly, and gradients reach any unit unchanged through the shortcut chain. He et al. trained a 1001-layer net this way. The change is one of placement: the layer types and counts are the same.

Slot fill. unit = pre-act basic | pre-act bottleneck · pre = norm + act · out act = none · identity reads raw · stem: last conv bare · final = norm + act. Stems and avg-down shortcuts come from the ResNet toolbox (the d nets use the deep stem and avg-down).

BiT: group norm + weight standardization

Design change. Every BN becomes a 32-group GN and every conv becomes a weight-standardized "StdConv". The stem uses a zero-pad and unpadded max pool. Widths are multiplied by ×1 to ×4.

Why. BiT pretrains on very large data (ImageNet-21k, JFT) with large batches spread over many devices, then fine-tunes on small downstream sets. BN's batch statistics cause problems at both ends: they need cross-device synchronization, and they shift under fine-tuning. GN+WS depends on no batch at all and matches BN's accuracy{{cite:wu2018gn,qiao2019}}. The width multiplier is the scaling knob. BiT's finding is that larger models need more data to pay off, and the reverse{{cite:kolesnikov2020}}.

EvoNorm ResNetV2

Each pre-activation "norm + act" pair becomes one EvoNorm-S0 layer{{cite:liu2020evo}}, which is batch-independent like GN and carries its own SiLU-like gate. In the algebra this is a change of norm kind whose activation comes with it. There is no new structure.

As a trunk: the hybrid ViT

The same kit also describes ResNetV2 without pre-activation: a normed stem with a SAME-padded pool, post-activation bottlenecks, weight-standardized SAME-padded convs, and no final norm or head. That is the trunk of the hybrid Vision Transformer (R50+ViT-B/16), which cuts its feature map into patches for a transformer {{cite:dosovitskiy2020}}. Here the conv lineage hands over to the transformer one: see Hybrid ViT, and From Units to Blocks for how the two halves of the algebra correspond.
