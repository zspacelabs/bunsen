ZONOS3's language model is a gated-DeltaNet hybrid like Qwen3.5, with two changes that make it the most unusual stack in bunsen: it has no residual stream, and most of its feed-forwards are a mixture of experts run in a narrow latent space.

Attention residuals

In a residual stream, sublayer l reads the sum of the embedding and every earlier output, each with weight one (the stream). ZONOS3's "full attention residuals" keep those outputs apart in a bank, and each sublayer reads its own mixture of them. The sublayer has a learned query vector; each source in the bank is RMS-normalized into a key; a softmax over the sources' scores gives the weights; and the sublayer reads the weighted sum of the sources. Its output is appended to the bank, never added to anything. The model's output is one last route through the whole bank. With 11 layers there are 23 routes (one before each of 22 sublayers, and one for the output), and the queries start at zero, which gives an even mixture.

The weights depend on each position's own sources, so every position chooses its own mixture, and the routing stays causal and needs no cache of its own. It generalizes the depth-weighted averages of DenseFormer{{cite:pagliardini2024}} from fixed learned weights to input-dependent ones. It gives the model a direct read of any earlier layer's output, without that output having been diluted in a growing sum.

The stack

11 layers of width 1024. Layers 3 and 7 (every fourth, counting from one) are global attention: 16 query heads and 4 key/value heads of width 32, a weightless QK-norm before RoPE, a learned per-head gain on the queries (its own temperature), and a headwise output gate. The other nine are gated DeltaNets with 4 heads of 128. Every sublayer reads through an RMS norm. The head scores all ten codebooks' 4098 entries at once, soft-capped at 15.

The latent mixture of experts

A mixture of experts replaces one feed-forward with many, and a router that sends each token to a few{{cite:shazeer2017}}. Parameters grow with the expert count, but compute only with the number chosen. ZONOS3's layer 0 has a dense SwiGLU; layers 1 to 10 each have 64 SwiGLU experts, of which a token uses 2. It runs them in a latent: the router reads the full 1024-wide input, but the token is first projected down to 256, the experts work at that width, and their weighted output is projected back up. That makes each expert small enough that 64 of them fit in a small model: 800M parameters in all, about 100M used per token.

The router balances its load without an auxiliary loss: a per-expert bias is added to the scores for choosing experts but not for weighting them, and is adjusted in training to even out the load{{cite:wang2024lossfree}}. There is no shared expert.
