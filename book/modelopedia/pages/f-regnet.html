RegNet{{cite:radosavovic2020}} changes the question. Instead of designing a network, Radosavovic et al. designed a design space: they started from a very general space of ResNeXt-like nets, sampled and trained many models, and narrowed the space step by step to the region where good models concentrate. The result is a rule. A RegNet is a ResNeXt-style trunk whose stage widths and depths come from four numbers instead of being listed by hand.

The fixed trunk

Every RegNet uses one 3×3 stride-2 stem with no pool, four stages of grouped bottleneck units (stride 2 on each stage's first mixer), and a pooled linear head. The unit is a ResNeXt bottleneck with two RegNet conventions: bottleneck ratio b (mixer width = unit width × b; 1 for X and Y, so there is no narrowing) and group size (channels per group, fixed across the net, rather than a group count).

The width rule

Per-unit widths come from a linear rule, which is then quantized to a geometric sequence. Runs of equal width become stages:

uj = w0 + wa·j,   sj = round( log(uj/w0) / log wm ),   wj = w0 · wmsj  (÷8)

What the paper found. In the good region of the space, widths grow roughly linearly with depth, the best models have about 20 blocks almost regardless of compute, and bottleneck ratio 1 with a stage-width multiplier near 2.5 works well. Inverted bottlenecks and depthwise convs did not help at their regime. The four parameters (plus group size) span a whole family across FLOP budgets, so each model's size label (200MF to 32GF) is a point on one curve and not a separate design.

One correction follows the rule: each mixer width is rounded to a multiple of the group size, and the unit width is recomputed from it. In RegNetY-3.2GF the generated 80, 568 and 1504 become 72, 576 and 1512 for groups of 24. The torchvision-trained variants round with a floor of 0.9 instead of to the nearest multiple.

RegNetX

The base design space: the trunk above, BN and ReLU, a 1×1 conv shortcut where a unit changes shape. No attention.

RegNetY: + squeeze-excite

RegNetX with an SE gate in every unit, placed after the mixer rather than at the unit's output, and sized at ¼ of the unit's input width. The paper's best models are RegNetYs. timm also hosts large RegNetY towers trained with self-supervision (SEER){{cite:goyal2021seer}} and weak supervision (SWAG){{cite:singh2022swag}}. Those are new weights for the same design.

RegNetV: pre-activated RegNetY

A timm design{{cite:timm}}: RegNetY units in pre-activation form, a bare stem conv, an activation after the last stage, and SiLU. Unlike ResNetV2, its identity shortcut reads the pre-activated input (the two conventions).

RegNetZ: inverted and linear

From the model-scaling follow-up{{cite:dollar2021}}, which studied how to scale nets by width, depth and resolution so that activations, and so run time, grow slowly. RegNetZ reverses three of RegNetX's choices:

  • Inverted bottleneck (b = 4): a narrow residual stream, widened 4× for the grouped mixer.
  • Linear outputs: no activation after the add, and one after the last stage instead (why).
  • No shortcut convs: a unit that changes shape has no residual at all.

It keeps SE at the mixer and uses SiLU. The _h variant adds a 1×1 conv to 1536 features before the pool (final layer).
