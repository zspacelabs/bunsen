Split attention ("Splat") is ResNeSt's mixer{{cite:zhang2020resnest}}. One grouped conv produces r candidate feature maps (the radix splits). An attention over their sum weighs them per channel, and the output is their weighted sum.

The operator

Ui = convi(x),  z = fc(GAP(Σi Ui)),  ac,· = softmaxi(zc,·),  yc = Σi ac,i Ui,c

This is exactly SK's fuse/select, with one change: SK's branches differ in receptive field, while ResNeSt's are identical in shape and differ only in their learned weights. The selection is therefore not "which scale" but "which of r learned views", a soft mixture of experts at the level of channels.

Cardinality × radix

Radix 1 is the degenerate case. There is nothing to choose between, the softmax becomes a sigmoid, and Splat reduces to a grouped conv followed by an SE gate. In that sense ResNeSt contains SE-ResNeXt. The attention width is r·C_in·¼ rounded to 8s, with a floor of 32.

In the algebra

Split attention is a mixer kind for bottleneck units. ResNeSt also always strides by an average pool (avd) placed before or after this mixer, which keeps the mixer at stride 1. DropBlock acts after the grouped conv's norm. A ResNeSt cannot also take a separate attention or anti-aliasing slot, since its mixer already fills both roles.
