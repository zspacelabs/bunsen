Every conv net in this part is a composition of the same five things:

model = head ∘ pool ∘ final ∘ stage4 ∘ stage3 ∘ stage2 ∘ stage1 ∘ stem

The rest of the book fills in the factors. This chapter sets out the shape, the slots, and how a family's own vocabulary (depth lists, cardinality, radix, RegNet's w₀, wa, wm) reduces to slot choices.

The macro shape

A residual conv net turns an image into a hierarchy of feature maps. The stem cuts the resolution by 4 and lifts the channels from 3 to about 64. Four stages follow. Each one usually halves the side and doubles the width, so the total stride reaches 32. The head then pools away space and classifies.

Inside a stage, the first unit does the reshaping (it strides and changes the channel count) and every later unit maps the feature space to itself:

The generic residual unit

All of the families' unit designs (basic, bottleneck, grouped, pre-activated, inverted, the attention variants, the three exotic mixers) are instances of one unit with optional slots:

y = actout( shortcut(x̃) + droppath( attnout( path(x̃) ) ) ),   x̃ = pre(x) or x

where the path is a chain of convs, one of which is the spatial mixer, the only conv with a spatial kernel larger than 1×1, and the one that strides.

Algebraic laws worth knowing

A few identities between combinators come up again and again in the family chapters. Several "new architectures" turn out to be rewrites of older ones under one of these.

  • Parallel paths are groups. k parallel bottleneck branches whose outputs are summed equal one bottleneck whose mixer is a k-group conv. This is ResNeXt's central observation{{cite:xie2017}}, and it is why "cardinality" is just the mixer's groups.
  • Small kernels compose into large reach. n stacked 3×3 convs have the reach of one (2n+1)×(2n+1) conv, with fewer parameters and more nonlinearity{{cite:simonyan2014}}. The deep stem and the Res2 hierarchy both use this.
  • Stride and dilation trade. Removing a stride and dilating every later conv by the same factor keeps each unit's receptive field and the weights' meaning, at higher resolution{{cite:yu2016,chen2017deeplab}}. This is output stride.
  • A softmax over branches is a point on a simplex. SK and split attention are the same fuse/select operator. SK's branches differ in receptive field and ResNeSt's in initialization; at radix 1 the operator degenerates to SE.
  • Pre-activation commutes the unit boundary. Moving the norm and activation from the end of one unit to the start of the next changes no layer, only where the identity is cut, and that alone makes the shortcut a pure identity{{cite:he2016id}}.

From a family's vocabulary to slots

Every family speaks its paper's language. A ResNet is a depth list and a stem kind. A ResNeXt adds cardinality and base width, a ResNeSt a radix, a RegNet four real numbers. In bunsen each family's policy lowers onto the one generic conv-net policy, and that policy is resolved into a fully explicit structure: every unit's channels, strides, dilations and slot contents.

The rest of the book follows the same split. Family chapters say which slots change and why. Combinator chapters say what a slot's kinds compute. The transformer half has a second generic model with its own slots, and From Units to Blocks maps them onto these.
