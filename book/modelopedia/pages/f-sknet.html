Selective Kernel Networks{{cite:li2019sk}} replace the spatial mixer with a selective kernel: two parallel paths with receptive fields 3 and 5, weighed per channel by an attention over their sum. Each unit chooses its kernel size for each input. Outside the mixer it is a ResNet or ResNeXt.

Design

Change. Mixer = selective kernel. It is the only exotic mixer that also fits basic units, where it replaces the first 3×3.

Why. Fixed kernel sizes per layer were a hand-picked hyperparameter, and Inception had shown that mixing several sizes helps. SK makes the mix dynamic through a fuse/select attention, so the effective receptive field adapts to object scale, which the paper links to how visual cortex neurons adapt their receptive fields.

Two shapes of the design exist in the kit:

  • SKResNet (basic units): the first 3×3 of each unit becomes the selective kernel, and the input is split across the two paths to keep the cost of a basic unit.
  • SKResNeXt (grouped bottlenecks): the grouped 3×3 becomes a selective kernel with grouped paths. This is the original paper's main model.

Lineage

SK's fuse/select operator is SE's squeeze-excite turned toward branches instead of channels{{cite:hu2018}}. ResNeSt keeps the operator and replaces SK's two kernel sizes with r identically shaped splits inside a cardinal group.
