bunsen builds dozens of model families: residual conv nets from ResNet to RegNet; vision transformers from ViT to Swin and the image towers of CLIP, SigLIP and DINOv2; language models from nanochat to Gemma 3 and Qwen3.5; and speech models that recognize (Whisper, Qwen3-ASR, Voxtral Realtime, Parakeet) and synthesize (Qwen3-TTS, ZONOS3). Read paper by paper they look like separate architectures, each with its own vocabulary. Read as structure they are four generic models, each a small set of sockets into which a family plugs its components, and all four are built from the same residual unit.

The Modelopedia follows that reading. Each family is described where it differs from its parent, with a circuit board that shows which sockets it filled and marks the ones it changed. Fine structure is left to the combinators the families are built from, and each combinator gets its own theory and geometric pictures. It documents architecture, not Rust APIs, and splits families only at real design changes, never by size or training recipe.

Four parts I · Conv nets: the residual unit, conv blocks, norms, spatial mixers, channel attention, shortcuts, stems and heads; ResNet, ResNeXt, ResNetV2, Res2Net, SKNet, ResNeSt, RegNet.
II · Vision transformers: patches and tokens, self-attention, the MLP, the residual sublayer, windows, merges, pools; ViT, DeiT, the multimodal towers, DINOv2, Swin.
III · Language models: embeddings and heads, causal attention, rotary positions, masks and windows, logit control, feed-forwards, norms, the gated DeltaNet, stream extras, decode state; nanochat, Qwen3, Gemma, Qwen3.5, ModernBERT, Laya.
IV · Speech: front ends, stems, audio encoders, the Conformer, bridges, transducers, codecs, language models over codes, routes and experts, streams; Whisper, Qwen3-ASR, Voxtral, Parakeet, Qwen3-TTS, ZONOS3.

The parts build on each other. A vision encoder's block is a conv net's residual unit with attention in the mixer; a language model's layer is that block with a causal mask and rotary positions; a speech model is a language model with a signal path attached. Wherever two parts describe the same idea from different sides, the chapters link to each other.
