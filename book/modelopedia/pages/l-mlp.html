The feed-forward is the channel mixer: the same small network applied to each position alone. It holds about two thirds of a dense model's non-embedding parameters, and is often read as the model's memory: its first layer's rows act as keys that match patterns in the stream, and its second layer's columns as the values written back when they fire{{cite:geva2021}}. The kits use two shapes, plain and gated, and four activations.

The plain MLP

The Transformer's feed-forward expands to 4D, applies a ReLU, and projects back{{cite:vaswani2017}}. Two kits keep that shape:

  • nanochat uses ReLU(x)², without biases. Squared ReLU came out of Primer's architecture search as one of the few changes that reliably improved language-model training{{cite:so2021}}; it is exactly zero for negative inputs, so the hidden layer is sparse, and smooth at the origin.
  • Laya's head layers are PyTorch's stock encoder layer: ReLU, 4D, biased.

Gated MLPs

A gated linear unit computes (φ(x Wgate) ⊙ x Wup) Wdown: two parallel projections to the hidden width, one through an activation, multiplied elementwise. The idea came from gated convolutional language models{{cite:dauphin2017}}; Shazeer showed that in the transformer's feed-forward it beats the plain MLP at equal parameters and compute, and named the variants by their activation{{cite:shazeer2020}}. To keep the parameter count of a 4D plain MLP, the hidden width shrinks to 8D/3.

  • SwiGLU (φ = SiLU): Qwen3 and Qwen3.5, as in PaLM and Llama.
  • GeGLU (φ = GELU{{cite:hendrycks2016}}): Gemma, with GELU's tanh approximation, and ModernBERT, with exact GELU. Gemma uses a far wider hidden layer than the parity rule: 8D in Gemma 1. ModernBERT's is narrow (1.5D in base), as befits an encoder tuned for inference speed.

The gate multiplies two learned projections, so the unit is quadratic in its input, and each hidden channel can be shut off by its own gate. That second, multiplicative path is the usual explanation for the gain.

Used by plain: nanochat (ReLU²), Laya's head (ReLU) · SwiGLU: Qwen3, Qwen3.5 · GeGLU: Gemma 1–3 (tanh), ModernBERT (exact)
