A codec frame is not one token but several: 16 codes for Qwen3-TTS, 10 for ZONOS3. A synthesizer's language model must predict that small, internally dependent object at every step. This chapter covers how a frame is fed in, and the two ways the kits factor its prediction.

Rows

Both models feed each frame as one position whose embedding is the sum of an embedding per codebook, plus a text embedding. Summing is cheap, keeps one position per frame, and is a natural fit for residual codes, which are themselves summed on decoding. The text column is how the text reaches the model: ZONOS3 gives every row a text id (the text first, then a pad), and Qwen3-TTS adds the next text token to each generated frame (its "interleaved" mode) or puts all the text first and pads after (its default). Speaker and condition rows replace the sum with a projected embedding or a learnt row per bucket.

Three layouts

Flattening every frame into K sequential tokens is exact but makes the sequence K times longer. The kits use the other two layouts:

  • The delay pattern (ZONOS3), as in MusicGen{{cite:copet2023}}: codebook k of frame f is written in row f + k. One step samples all K heads at once, independently, but by the time codebook k of a frame is sampled, codebooks 0…k−1 of that frame were sampled in earlier rows and are in context. The cost is K − 1 rows of lag, and a stop rule that runs K rows past the end-of-audio symbol to complete the last frames.
  • A depth decoder (Qwen3-TTS): the main model steps once per frame and predicts only codebook 0. A small second decoder then runs within the frame, over the codebook axis: its context is the main model's hidden state and codebook 0's embedding, and it writes codebooks 1 to 15 one after another, with a table and a head of its own for each. The idea is the RQ-Transformer's{{cite:lee2022rq}}, used for speech by Moshi{{cite:defossez2024}}. It is exact within the frame and costs one 5-layer decode per frame.

Sampling

Both models sample rather than search. Speech has many valid realizations, and the argmax path is flat and repetitive. Qwen3-TTS samples at temperature 0.9 with top-k 50 and a mild repetition penalty (1.05) on codebook 0. ZONOS3 samples every codebook at 0.75 with top-k 80 and top-p 0.69, and a strong repetition penalty (2.4) over the last 96 rows on the first six codebooks only, the ones that carry content. A take ends when the model emits its end-of-audio code.
