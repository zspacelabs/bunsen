ZONOS3-Mini is Zyphra's compact synthesizer{{cite:zyphra2026zonos3}}: 800M parameters, about 100M active per step, speaking nine languages at 44.1 kHz. Against Qwen3-TTS it changes nearly every socket: how a frame's codebooks are laid out, the stack, the codec, and how a voice enters.

ZONOS3-Mini

Design change.

  • Rows and layout. Each row is 11 ids (ten codebooks and a text id), summed through their own tables, with the codebooks staggered by the delay pattern: one step samples all ten heads at once.
  • Prompt. A speaker (reference codes from a clip, or a projected speaker embedding), one row per condition (loudness, bandwidth, speaking rate, each bucketed), the text, and a few frames of silence to start from.
  • Stack. 11 layers of width 1024: nine gated DeltaNets and two global attention layers, joined by attention residuals instead of a residual stream, with a dense SwiGLU in the first layer and a latent mixture of experts (64 experts, top-2) in the other ten.
  • Codec. Ten codebooks of 4096 at 15 Hz; five windowed causal attention stages joined by patch-ups up to 240 Hz; ConvNeXt blocks; two ISTFT heads, mixed and resampled to 44.1 kHz.

Why. Each change trades a little exactness for a lot of speed. The delay pattern needs one model step per frame, where a depth decoder adds fifteen small ones. DeltaNet layers keep the per-step cost constant over a long take. The latent MoE gives the model the capacity of 800M parameters at the cost of 100M. And an ISTFT head makes the last ×184 of upsampling an inverse FFT. Attention residuals let each sublayer read exactly the earlier outputs it needs; one reading is that a small model benefits most from not diluting them in a sum.

The speaker encoder

To clone a voice from a clip without its codes, ZONOS3 has a speaker encoder from the speaker-recognition literature: a Kaldi filterbank, AntSpeaker's MECT-B2 network (residual convolution stages, each followed by a small transformer layer over the flattened channels whose feed-forward is a soft mixture of four experts{{cite:puigcerver2024}}), a learned mix of the stages, attentive statistics pooling{{cite:okabe2018}} to one 192-dimensional vector, and a projection that replaces one prompt row. The stock voices use reference codes instead, which give the model the voice's actual sound to continue from.
