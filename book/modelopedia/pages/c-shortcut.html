Why average-down?

The pool is 2×2 with the unit's stride, in ceil mode and not counting padding. In a dilated stage (stride 1 by output stride) it becomes a 2×2 stride-1 pool with one row and column of padding, so the shortcut still smooths the input the way it would have at stride 2.

Projection as a change of basis

A 1×1 projection plus norm is a learned linear map between the two stages' feature spaces. Within a stage the residual stream keeps one basis, and identity shortcuts preserve it. At a stage boundary it is re-expressed in a wider space. In a pre-activated unit the projection reads the pre-activated input, and in timm's pre-activation bottleneck it has no norm of its own: the next unit's pre-norm takes that role.
