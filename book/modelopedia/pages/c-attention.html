Channel attention rescales the channels of a feature map by gains computed from the map itself:

y = x ⊙ σ( f( meanh,w x ) )

A spatial mean (the squeeze) summarizes each channel into one number. A small function f maps that C-vector to C logits, and their sigmoids gate the channels (the excite). Every variant in the lineage has this form and differs only in f and in where the gate is placed.

Squeeze-and-excitation

The squeeze gives the gate global context: every channel's gain depends on the whole image, which a 3×3 conv stack only sees after many layers. The cost is tiny, about 2C²/r parameters and no spatial work.

Geometry: an input-conditioned diagonal

ECA: local channel interaction

ECA's argument is that SE's dimensionality reduction hurts: the bottleneck forces the gates through a rank C/r subspace. A banded map keeps full rank at k parameters per unit, but assumes that channel order carries meaning, which holds only to the extent that training makes it so.

Placement

In the generic unit the attention is one slot with a placement flag; the kind and the sizing rule are the only other degrees of freedom. A family that "adds SE" (SE-ResNet, SE-ResNeXt, RegNetY, ResNet-RS) fills this slot and changes nothing else.
